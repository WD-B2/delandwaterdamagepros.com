What to say when you call

A few details help the person on the phone give safety steps and prepare the right crew. You do not need to know technical terms, just describe what you see.

  • Your address and the town you are in.
  • Where the water is coming from, if you know.
  • Whether the water is still spreading, and how deep it is.
  • Which rooms or floors are wet, and whether it reached outlets or appliances.
  • Whether the water came from a supply line, a drain, a roof or outdoors.
  • Whether you are on city sewer or septic, and city water or a well.

While you wait for a crew

Stop the source if you can, usually by closing the main water valve. Turn off power to wet areas only from a dry breaker you can reach safely, and call the power utility or 911 for sparks or a gas smell. Take photos of the damage before anything is moved, and keep children and pets out of water that came from a drain or outdoors.
