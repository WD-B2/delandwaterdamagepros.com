About Deland Water Damage Pros

A Deland-based water damage restoration service for homes and businesses in West Volusia. Here is how we work and what you can expect when you call.

24/7 line
(386) 699-0141
Call now

Deland Water Damage Pros is a water damage restoration service based at 305 E International Speedway Blvd in Deland, Florida. We answer the line 24/7 and send crews to homes and businesses in Deland and the towns around it. Our work covers water removal, structural drying and repair after leaks, floods, storms and sewage backups.

People call us on some of the worst days a building can have, so we try to make the first call simple. You describe what you see, we ask a few safety questions and we explain the next steps before anything is booked.

How we work

Every job starts with safety and the source of the water. Crews check for electrical risks, confirm the leak is stopped and then inspect with moisture meters and thermal imaging so the plan is based on readings and not guesses. You hear the scope and the price drivers before work starts.

Drying is monitored, not assumed. Crews return to take readings and adjust equipment until the structure meets its drying goals, and the log is shared with you for your records and your insurance adjuster.

What we cover

We handle the full range of residential and commercial water losses, from standing water and burst pipes to ceiling leaks and sewage backups. We also handle mold remediation when moisture has gone unchecked. See the full services page for every service and what each involves.

We are not a roofer, a plumber or a waterproofing contractor. When a job needs one of those, we say so and coordinate the timing so the drying work and the repair do not collide.

Where we work

The line covers Deland and nearby towns, including Glenwood, Orange City, Lake Helen, Cassadaga and De Leon Springs. Our service area page explains how we group these towns and how to check whether your address is a fit.

How we handle information and pricing

We explain pricing in plain terms. The cost of a water damage job depends on the size of the wet area, the water type, the materials involved and the days of drying equipment. We do not quote firm figures before seeing the water, and we do not promise what your insurer will pay.

We do not publish customer reviews or invented statistics on this site. If a number appears, it comes from a public source that is named on the page. Local facts, such as flood zones and permits, come from county and state sources.

What a typical call looks like

The person who answers asks where you are, where the water is coming from and whether it is still spreading. They also ask about electrical risks, because water near outlets changes what is safe to do. You then get plain steps for the next few minutes, such as closing the main valve and lifting valuables, while a crew is arranged.

If the cause is a roof, a plumbing line or an HVAC drain, we tell you which trade to call and coordinate with them. Drying should not start before the source is stopped, and repairs should not start before the structure is dry.

What you get at the end of a job

You should finish a job with a written scope, a drying log with moisture readings, photos of the work and an invoice that matches the scope. Those records support an insurance claim and help if you sell the home later. If anything is unclear, ask us to walk you through it.

We also tell you what we saw that you should watch, such as a slab that may need a plumber's leak test or a roofline that needs repair before the next storm. Those notes are practical and plain, and they come from the readings taken on site.
