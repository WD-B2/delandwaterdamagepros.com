A leaking water heater is an emergency because the tank can release its full volume quickly, so shut the cold supply valve above the tank and call the 24/7 line. Turn off power at the breaker for an electric unit or set a gas unit to pilot or off, and stay out of the water if it has reached the heater wiring. Photograph the leak point and the water line before cleanup.

Water heater leak cleanup means removing the water, drying the floor, wall bases and cabinets near the unit and repairing what cannot be dried. A tank usually fails at the bottom seam or at a fitting, and the water travels out across the slab. In Deland, heaters sit in garages, hall closets and attics, so the damage pattern depends on where yours is.

Water heater leak emergency: first steps

Find the cold water inlet valve at the top of the tank and turn it clockwise. If the heater is electric, switch off its breaker, and if it is gas, turn the gas control to off. Same-day response is offered when available for active leaks once the line has your address.

Gas, electricity and hot water

A gas smell means leave the house and call the gas utility or 911 from outside. Do not flip switches or use a phone indoors.

Water from the relief valve can scald. Stay clear of discharge pipes and wait for the tank to cool before touching it.

Why water heaters leak in Florida homes

Tanks often last around a decade, and hard Florida water speeds sediment buildup that wears the lining. If your unit is in that age range, a plumber can inspect it before it fails, and a drain pan with a line to the outside is cheap insurance.

Attic water heaters are common in some Central Florida homes. A leak up there drips onto the ceiling below and soaks insulation first, so the first sign may be a stain in a hallway. The ceiling water damage page explains how that repair works.

In garage installations on a slab, water spreads across the floor and under the wall where it can reach drywall and stored items. Lift boxes onto shelves and save wet cardboard for the adjuster to see.

Signs your water heater is leaking

Water at the base of the tank

Puddles or rust streaks around the bottom are the clearest sign.

Rusty hot water

Tinted water suggests the tank lining is failing.

Rumbling or popping

Sediment boiling on the bottom can signal a tank near the end.

Damp drywall near the closet

Moisture at wall bases means water has traveled past the pan.

How crews clean up a water heater leak

Crews extract standing water, remove wet baseboards or drywall edges if moisture has wicked in, and map moisture with meters and thermal imaging. Air movers and LGR dehumidifiers dry the area. Cabinets and stored items are checked for hidden wetness.

A plumber replaces or repairs the heater. We coordinate so equipment does not block the plumber and the drying schedule stays on track.

What affects water heater leak cleanup price

  1. How much water spread and how long it sat.
  2. Floor type, such as tile, concrete, vinyl plank or carpet.
  3. Whether drywall and baseboards must be removed.
  4. Drying equipment days.
  5. Whether the heater is in a finished closet or attic that needs rebuilding.

Insurance and water heater leaks

A sudden tank failure is commonly covered for resulting water damage, though the heater itself may not be. A slow leak over months can be treated differently. Notify your carrier within one year under Florida Statute 627.70132.

The industry standard behind the work

Professional water damage restoration follows a published industry document, the ANSI/IICRC S500. It sets out how a loss is assessed, how affected materials are classified and how drying or cleanup is documented.

Ask any company how it applies that standard on your job. A clear answer should cover readings taken, equipment placed and the records you will receive. This page describes the approach, and it is not a claim about any individual certificate.

Is it safe to touch a leaking water heater?

Turn off power or gas first. Water around electric elements can be live, and relief valve water can be scalding.

Can a leaking water heater cause mold?

Yes if the area stays damp for more than a day or two. Prompt drying of wall bases and cabinets prevents it.

Do I need a plumber too?

Yes. A plumber repairs or replaces the heater. We handle the water and drying.
