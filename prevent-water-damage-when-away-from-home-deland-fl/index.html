Preventing Water Damage When You Are Away From Home in Deland, FL

A leak in an empty house runs for days before anyone sees it. This checklist helps you protect the house before you go.

24/7 line
(386) 699-0141
Call now

By the Deland Water Damage Pros team

An empty house gives a leak days or weeks to do damage, and a small failure can become a large loss. Taking an hour before a trip to close valves, check hoses and set up alerts costs little and protects floors, walls and your insurance claim. This guide gives a checklist for short trips and for longer absences.

Before you leave: the shutoff plan

Shut off the main water valve for any trip longer than a few days, and open a faucet to drain the lines. That removes pressure from supply lines to toilets, sinks, the water heater and appliances, the most common failure points. If you cannot shut off the main because irrigation or a pool needs it, shut the valves behind the washing machine and the dishwasher.

For longer absences, switch the water heater to vacation mode or off at the breaker. A tank that runs empty can overheat and damage the elements.

Add alerts and a human check

Water sensors that send an alert to your phone are inexpensive and catch a leak within minutes. Place them under the water heater, behind the washing machine, under sinks and near the AC air handler. Some shutoff valves close automatically when a sensor trips.

Ask a neighbor or friend to check the house weekly, and give them the shutoff location and our number. A quick walk-through catches a stain, a smell or a drip long before a bill does.

Storm season adds its own steps

The hurricane season runs June 1 to November 30, and Hurricane Ian in September 2022 and Hurricane Milton in October 2024 both reached Volusia County. If you leave during that window, clear gutters, secure outdoor items and photograph the house for your records. Ask your insurer whether coverage conditions apply to vacant homes.

Local tips for Deland

The Volusia County Fair and Expo Center in Deland served as a shelter during Hurricane Milton, so plan your route and your return date around storm forecasts. Check the weather before you leave and on the day you return.

City of Deland Utilities provides water and wastewater and has an emergency contact for broken water mains and sewer backups. Save the number, 386-626-7051, in your phone and in your neighbor's.

A checklist for the day you leave

Walk the house once with a list. Check under each sink, look at the water heater, test that the AC drain flows and feel the ceiling under any upstairs bathroom. This ten-minute walk finds many problems that would otherwise show up while you are away.

Set the thermostat to a steady setting, since an AC that is switched off in summer lets humidity climb and invites mold. Leave interior doors open so air can move, and keep closet doors ajar.

If you come home to water

Shut off the main valve, photograph the damage and call the 24/7 line. Do not enter if water is deep or has touched outlets. Our water extraction crews and burst pipe cleanup pages explain what happens next.

Seasonal residents in Lake Helen and Cassadaga often have older homes with older plumbing, so schedule a plumber check before a long absence.

Short trips versus a long absence

For a weekend, closing the washing machine valves, checking the AC drain and asking a neighbor to look in is enough. For two weeks or more, shut off the main valve, drain the lines and put the water heater in vacation mode. For a season, add a plumber check before you leave and a weekly walk-through while you are gone.

Seasonal residents often return to surprises such as a stained ceiling or a musty room. A set of dated photos from the day you left makes it easy to see what changed and helps if you file a claim.

Insurance and vacant homes

Some policies limit coverage for homes left vacant or unoccupied for long periods, and the definitions vary. Ask your agent how many days away triggers a condition, and whether you need to keep heat or water on. Florida Statute 627.70132 sets a one year notice window for property claims.

Questions we hear in Deland

Should I shut off my water when I leave for a week?

For trips longer than a few days, yes. It removes pressure from the lines that fail most often.

What if my irrigation system needs water?

Turn off the timer for the trip, or shut off the valves behind appliances at least.

Will insurance cover damage in a vacant house?

Some policies limit coverage after a number of days, so ask your agent about vacancy conditions.

Who watches the house if I leave during hurricane season?

Ask a neighbor to check it, and share the shutoff location. Check forecasts before you go.
