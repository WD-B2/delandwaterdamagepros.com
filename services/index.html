Water Damage Services in Deland, FL

Not sure which service fits? Start with what you can see. Standing water means extraction, a stain on a ceiling points to a leak above it and a musty smell with no visible source usually means hidden moisture. The line is answered 24/7, and the person on the phone can tell you which page and crew fit.

Each service page explains the cause, how crews assess and fix it, the choices you have and what affects the price. Our most requested services are flood damage cleanup, water extraction and commercial water damage restoration.

Pipes and plumbing

Supply lines, water heaters and slab pipes fail without much warning, and the water is usually clean when it starts. The goal is to stop the spread and dry the cavities before they grow mold.

  • Burst Pipe Water Damage Cleanup
  • Frozen Pipe Water Damage Restoration
  • Water Heater Leak Cleanup
  • Slab Leak Water Damage Cleanup

Appliances and HVAC

Appliance and AC leaks run slowly and hide behind cabinets, in attics and under floors. These pages explain how crews find the wet area and what gets saved.

  • Appliance Leak Water Damage Cleanup
  • AC Leak Water Damage Repair
  • Kitchen Water Damage Restoration

Weather and building envelope

Rain, wind, river rise and storm surge bring outside water in through roofs, doors and low spaces. The work starts with drying and ends with repair once the structure is dry.

  • Flood Damage Restoration and Cleanup
  • Ceiling Water Damage Repair
  • Crawl Space Water Damage Cleanup
  • Storm Water Damage Cleanup
  • Basement Water Damage Cleanup

Water removal and drying

Removing standing water is the first step. Drying the structure and handling sewage safely decide whether the rest of the house stays sound.

  • Water Extraction and Emergency Water Removal
  • Sewage Backup Cleanup
  • Structural Drying and Dehumidification

Floors and walls

Hardwood, carpet and drywall each dry differently, so the plan changes with the surface. These pages explain what can be saved and what is replaced.

  • Hardwood Floor Water Damage Repair
  • Carpet Water Damage Restoration
  • Wall and Drywall Water Damage Repair

Commercial

Businesses need a plan that protects inventory, tenants and downtime, with after-hours work when needed.

  • Commercial Water Damage Restoration

Mold

Mold follows moisture, and most Deland mold starts with a leak. Our mold page covers containment, removal, testing and clearance, including what a lender may ask for in a sale.

  • Mold Remediation
